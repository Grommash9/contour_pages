Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026

Introduction

Contour ("we", "our", or "us") is an iOS application created by Oleksandr Prudnikov. This Privacy Policy explains how Contour handles your information.

The short version: Contour does not collect any personal data. Everything happens on your device.

Information We Collect

Contour does not collect any personal data. There are:

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No ads.
  • No third-party SDKs.

Camera Access

Camera access is used only to show the live camera preview and to capture photos, entirely on your device. The camera feed is never recorded, transmitted, or shared.

Photo Library Access

Photo library access is used to let you pick reference photos and to save the captures and GIFs you create. Contour never uploads your photos — nothing leaves your device.

Data Sharing

No data is shared with anyone. Contour does not operate any servers, and your photos and creations stay on your iPhone unless you choose to share them yourself.

Children's Privacy

Contour does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will reflect any changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

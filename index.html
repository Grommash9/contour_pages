Who It's For

When "Close Enough" Isn't Good Enough

Saw a photo on Instagram you want to recreate? Someone posing at a statue, a doorway, a famous building — load their photo as your reference, line the contours up on the real scene, and stand exactly where they stood. Match the shot instead of guessing at it.

It works the same way for fitness and body progress shots, home projects and renovations, plants and restorations — anywhere framing matters and "close enough" isn't good enough.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Contour free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your device. Captures and GIFs save to your Photos library.

Do I have to take photos inside the app?

No. Shoot in Contour, or use your normal camera and pick photos later for Compare or GIF.

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Is it available in my language?

Contour follows your phone's language and is available in English, Spanish, and Russian.

When is the AI feature coming?

It's in the works. The current version focuses on aligning, comparing, and making GIFs.
